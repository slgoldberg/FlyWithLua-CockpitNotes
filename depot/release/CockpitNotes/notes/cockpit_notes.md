# Cockpit Notes & Scratchpad

Welcome to **Cockpit Notes** for FlyWithLua!

## Quick Start
* **Modes:** Use the toolbar above to switch between **Write** (distraction-free editor), **Split** (side-by-side editing and live rendered preview), and **Preview** (clean briefing document).
* **Zoom:** Use `[-]` and `[+]` or the mouse wheel to dynamically zoom text from **50% to 200%**. Click `↺` to toggle between 100% baseline and your previous zoom.
* **Aviation Templates:** Click the dropdown above to load pre-formatted checklists, ATIS copy pads, and IFR CRAFT clearances.
* **Multiple Notes:** Select, create, and save notes from the note selector in the top right.
